# Sponsors

Sponsorships keep claude-palace maintained. If it earns its keep on your machine, this is how to send some back.

## Why this exists

Palace is a one-developer project. I use it every day across my own work, and the public version stays free. Sponsorships pay for the time I spend fixing bugs other people hit, reviewing PRs, and shipping improvements that aren't on my own critical path. There's no plan to gate features behind sponsorship and there won't be.

Palace runs entirely on your own machine and bills against your own Claude Code auth. Sponsoring doesn't subsidize anyone else's compute and it doesn't unlock any cloud-hosted version. There isn't one.

## Tiers

### Hobbyist · $5/mo
You run palace on your own machine for personal projects. Send a few bucks if you want to keep it going. Only perk is knowing you helped.

### Pro · $25/mo
You use palace for client work or your day job. Your name lands in this file.

### Startup · $99/mo
Small teams running palace as part of their stack. Issues you open get triaged before the open queue. Listing in this file included.

### Company · $249/mo
Logo in the `README` and this file, linked to your site. Same priority triage as the Startup tier. Good fit if your devs use palace daily and you want that visible.

## What "priority triage" actually means

If you sponsor at $99 or above and open an issue, I'll respond within a few business days with either a fix, a workaround, or a clear "I won't do this and here's why." Same triage as I'd give a paying client. It is not a 24-hour SLA and it is not feature delivery on demand.
